Three useful terms for today

Build confidence one clear definition at a time.

📈 Beginner

Profit

The money left after a business subtracts all its costs from the money it earned.

Example If a bakery earns $1,000 and spends $700, its profit is $300.

💧 Beginner

Cash flow

The movement of money entering and leaving a business over a period of time.

Example Paying a supplier sends cash out, while a customer payment brings cash in.
Practice this term →
🤝 Intermediate

Stakeholder

A person or group affected by a company's decisions, actions, or performance.

Example Employees, customers, investors, and local communities can all be stakeholders.
